# How to make reels travel: the evidence-based playbook (v2, 2026-10-28)

**Sources:** 280 findings by 5 research agents (`findings/S1-S5.jsonl`). Most carry a source URL and a verbatim quote. 39 have no quote, and some pages were read only through a press relay (see each finding's notes). IDs in brackets point to the findings.

**Fact-check:** v2 applies all 66 FIX wordings and the 7 FAIL corrections from `FACT-CHECK.md` (separate agent; 29 of 29 key quotes re-found on the live pages). That was a single pass, not cross-model.

**Grades:**
- **A:** the platform or the paper itself says it.
- **B:** press quoting a platform head, a named practitioner (including practitioners' own studies such as Galloway's), or a vendor's blog. Vendor sources are marked "vendor".
- **LEAD:** a tip with no data.
- **Labels:** "ads" means the evidence comes from ad research and may not transfer to organic reels. "Our inference" means no source says it.

## The short version
1. **Keep people watching.** Watching is a named top-tier signal on all three platforms:
   - Instagram lists watch time and retention [S1-01] and names watch time, likes and sends as its top three [S1-07].
   - TikTok names finishing a longer video as a strong signal [S2-03, S2-07].
   - YouTube says its surveys measure satisfaction, not just watch time [S2-46].
   - No platform publishes weights or a ranking order.
2. **Make things people send.** Mosseri says, in his experience, sends per reach correlate with overall reach more than anything else [S1-09]. Sends count slightly more for non-followers and likes slightly more for followers [S1-08], and he said the same in May 2026 [S1-10] (B, press quoting Mosseri). So make things people forward to a friend (our inference).
3. **Strangers see it in a small test first.** Among non-followers, a reel is first shown to a small audience Instagram predicts will enjoy it; the size is not stated [S1-02] (A, Feb 2025). Meta says the vast majority of Reels engagement is increasingly likely to happen on day one [S1-14] (B, press relaying an earnings call).
4. **Be original.** On Instagram, reposts, other apps' watermarks and aggregator accounts are less likely to be recommended [S1-21, S1-22, S1-24, S1-25, S1-29]. TikTok makes unoriginal or reused content ineligible for the For You feed [S2-11, S2-15, S2-17].
5. **Small levers:**
   - Mosseri says hashtags and long captions do little for Instagram reach [S1-38, S1-39, S1-46] (B).
   - Instagram's FAQ ties posting time to when your audience is active [S1-49] (A).
   - In Galloway's YouTube Shorts sample, hold rather than length tracked performance [S4-09] (B).
6. **Slovene gets no platform help we could confirm.** Slovene is not named in Meta AI reel translation (Jan and Jul 2026) [S5-11, S5-12], nor in Edits bilingual captions (Jul 2026, trade-press list) [S5-17]. The Instagram and TikTok auto-caption lists we opened date from 2022 and don't include Slovene [S5-14, S5-15]; current support is unknown. So we burn in our own captions, and an English version is a separate post.
7. **Test with Trial reels.** Trial reels go to non-followers first, and their metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) appear about 24 h after sharing [S1-32, S1-33] (A, Dec 2024).

## 2. Rules for the director, step by step

### 2.1 The idea
- **Choose ideas that are useful, surprising, interesting, or that provoke awe or anger.** Evidence:
  - New York Times articles on the most-emailed list in 2008 were more often high-arousal (awe, anger, anxiety), more positive, practically useful, interesting and surprising [S3-33..S3-36] (A). That is news shared by email; transfer to reels is untested.
  - Sad, low-arousal content was shared less [S3-33] (A).
  - Interest was the strongest predictor of self-reported willingness to share science news [S3-37, S3-38] (A).
  - The arousal lab experiment failed to replicate [S3-39], and a German replication found an inverted U for positivity [S3-40]. Treat all of these as tendencies.
  - "Funny" and "about their city" are our inference.
- **Series:** keep the visual layout identical from episode to episode, which Hoyos says helps bingeability [S4-23] (B, one practitioner).
- **Audience size in Slovenia:** ad-tool reach is about 0.7M on Instagram and 0.5M adults on TikTok [S5-01, S5-02]. That's an upper-bound proxy, not audience size (DataReportal, Oct 2025 data). Instagram is the most popular platform for following influencers in Slovenia [S5-10] (B, n=1,262). Whether an English angle widens reach is untested (section 4).

### 2.2 The hook (first ~3 s)
- **Treat the intro like a thumbnail** [S4-08]. Make the hook visual so it works without sound [S4-11]. Keep it to about 3 s or less, centred, with no more than about three objects [S4-12] (B: Galloway and Hoyos, YouTube Shorts practitioners).
- **Show what the video contains; don't announce it** [S4-02] (B, long-form memo). A long-form guide states the specific promise by about 15-30 s [S4-24] (B). Neither source tested 15-60 s reels. For reels, deliver within the first seconds (our inference).
- **Energy:** one long-form editor defines early retention as energy and advises opening high and letting it dip [S4-28] (B, single practitioner, no data).
- **What we know about early drop-off:**
  - Instagram reports who stays past the first 3 seconds (View Rate, now Skip Rate) [S1-63, S1-64]. No platform states a cut-off, so treat early drop-off as a risk, not a rule (our inference).
  - In a data-donation study of 347 TikTok users (52% from Africa, 3% from Europe) covering 9.2M recommended views, 24% of views were skipped before 20% of the video's duration [S3-18] (A, CHI 2024). That measures share of duration, not seconds 1-3.
  - Ads: 65% of those who pass 3 s reach 10 s [S3-04] (A, ads, no method). 90% of TikTok ad-recall impact is captured within 6 s [S3-11] (A, ads).
- **Hook formulas** (curiosity gap, contrarian, specificity with a number, "you" hooks, mid-story open, myth-buster, confession) are catalogued in `raw/S4.md`, and the director writes 3 variants per script [S4-36..S4-47].
  - **All are LEAD.** They come from one repo with unverifiable provenance, and no A/B data on hook types was found.
  - One podcast co-host and one paid-ads vendor claim recognisable formulas wear out [S4-34, S4-35] (LEAD). Rotate them, and judge by your own numbers.
- **Curiosity gap:** across 8,977 news-headline A/B tests (Upworthy, 2013-2015), click-through was highest when the headline revealed a middling amount of information [S3-44] (A). Users don't reliably click more on headlines flagged as clickbait [S3-45] (A). Both measure headline clicks, not video holds. So tease the payoff and name its subject (our inference).

### 2.3 Script and structure
- **Chain beats with "but" and "therefore", not "and then"** [S4-14] (B).
- **Keep opening new problems.** "It's not necessarily about pace of editing, it's pace of new problems" [S4-25] (B).
- **Explainers:** a list of 3, 5 or 7 things, with a clear promise and a narrow scope [S4-26] (B).
- **Pacing:** quick hook, medium middle with one peak, quick end [S4-15] (B). Every scene should change what the viewer feels [S4-28] (B). No dull moments [S4-04] (B, MrBeast memo).
- **End right after the payoff, and don't announce the ending** [S4-06, S4-27] (B).
  - Hoyos trimmed one dead second at the end and her retention went from 83% to 88% [S4-17] (B, one anecdote).
  - Rewatches can push retention above 100%. In her example, a Short with 70% retention on its first 100K views reached about 10M views because viewers kept returning [S4-18] (B, one anecdote). A loop ending (the last line flows into the first) is our application of that.
- **Write simply:** Hoyos aims for a 5th-grade reading level [S4-19] (B; the checkers are English-only).
- **Write the last line before filming** [S4-13] (B).

### 2.4 Edit
- **Cut pauses, fillers and retakes.** Watch time is a named signal on Instagram [S1-01] and TikTok [S2-07]; TikTok says to trim lulls [S2-07] (A).
- **Burn in captions:**
  - Meta's internal tests: captions raised video view time by 12% [S3-06] (A, ads, pre-2020).
  - A 2015 review of more than 100 studies found captions improve comprehension of, attention to and memory for video, mostly instructional video and learners [S3-23] (A, not social video).
  - Instagram's Sept 2026 guide: add captions even when using text overlays [S1-47] (B).
  - We found no confirmed platform auto-captions for Slovene [S5-14, S5-15], so ours come from `shared/tools/sl-stt`.
- **Audio still matters.** Meta's Reels-ads page says over 75% of Reels views are sound on (no method) [S3-08] (A, ads page). The famous 85% silent Facebook figure, in the 2016 Digiday article we opened, comes from publishers' own data and an agency, with no independent measurement [S3-25] (B). Reels ads with both music and voiceover scored 15 points higher on positive response [S3-10] (A, ads).
- **On-screen text:** TikTok's ads guidance recommends 5-10 words per second when using text [S2-34] (A, ads).
- **Lo-fi is fine.** TikTok's ads guidance says a DIY, not over-polished style fits in with user content [S2-35] (A, ads). TikTok-first ads (lo-fi, 9:16, with people) saw clicks or shares rise nearly 3.3x versus other ads, with the attributes bundled [S3-14] (A, ads, 2022).
- **Faces and talking heads:** Instagram photos with faces were 38% more likely to get likes [S3-22] (B, 2014, photos not video). TikTok's ads guide says to avoid static talking heads, and that quick demos with captions often beat talking-head promos, with no data given [S2-32] (A, ads). So: move, show, and cut to B-roll (our inference).
- **Watermarks and quality:**
  - Never leave another app's watermark; your own logo is fine [S1-29, S1-30] (A/B).
  - Edits exports carry none [S1-55] (A). Mosseri: "For now, Edits does help a little bit with reach" [S1-54] (B).
  - Low-resolution reels are made less visible, so upload at full resolution [S1-31] (A).
- **Cut frequency:** we found no study measuring it against retention; the one related result is a lab EEG blink-rate study [S3-26] (B). Don't add cuts for their own sake; add new information.

### 2.5 Length
- No platform page we opened names an ideal organic length. Instagram says it doesn't want to penalise longer videos [S1-11] (B).
- Galloway's 2023 YouTube Shorts study: Shorts whose average view duration was above 50 s averaged 4.1M views, and he concludes that holding viewers, not length alone, is the goal [S4-09] (B, one practitioner, YouTube only, observational).
- Instagram's guide, as reported, calls 15-60 s the sweet spot for most creative formats [S1-19] (B). Reels up to 3 min can be recommended [S1-16] (A). "Every extra second must add something" is our advice.
- Hoyos's own experience: her YouTube Shorts worked best at about 34 s, TikTok favoured dense 10-20 s videos, and Instagram videos often carry subtitles because of the mute feature [S4-22, S4-16] (B, no data).

### 2.6 Caption, text and posting
- **Caption:** relevant keywords [S1-42, S1-45] and up to five targeted hashtags [S1-40]. Mosseri says caption length doesn't affect reach much either way [S1-46] (B). Whether Slovene keywords are searchable is unknown [S1-44].
- **Topic:** TikTok names voiceover, captions and on-screen text as ways to make the topic clear [S2-21] (A). Instagram says to put keywords "in your content, your caption, your bio, and your hashtags" [S1-45]; it doesn't say it reads speech or on-screen text.
- **Collab posts** can be recommended in the Reels tab, Explore and Feed [S1-50] (A, 2023). Mosseri says ranking leans slightly towards the account that sent the invite [S1-51] (B). For the café: Collab with Kaval, suppliers, guests and local creators (our inference).
- **Trending audio:** Instagram's FAQ says it can impact distribution [S1-48] (A). YouTube's blog says it increases the likelihood of wider distribution [S2-55] (A).
- **Timing:** post when your audience is awake and most active (check Insights) [S1-49]. Meta says most Reels engagement increasingly happens on day one [S1-14] (B).
- **Stories:** Mosseri says posting a reel to your Stories helps a bit [S1-70] (B). Reposting feed posts to Stories won't meaningfully change reach, and hack-type tactics "sometimes work, usually don't" [S1-69] (B).

### 2.7 Test and measure
- **Trial reels:**
  - Shown to non-followers first; metrics appear about 24 h after sharing.
  - Instagram can auto-share a trial with followers if it performs well within the first 72 hours (opt-in) [S1-32..S1-35] (A).
  - Mosseri: trials get "less reach, almost always" [S1-37] (B, via Buffer). So compare trials with trials.
  - Per Buffer, trials need a professional account [S1-36] (B, vendor). That's Stepan's task 7.
- **What to read, in this order:**
  1. **Skip rate or View Rate**, who stays past 3 s [S1-63, S1-64]: this judges the hook. Check that it appears for trial reels. Instagram's Apr 2026 Insights update didn't cover trial reels [S1-66], and its trial-reels post lists only views, likes, comments and shares [S1-33]. If skip rate is missing, compare views, shares and watch time instead.
  2. **Average watch time and the retention curve:** look for the drop-off point [S1-61, S1-65, S2-25]. This judges the script and the edit.
  3. **Sends (shares) per reach** [S1-07, S1-09]: Mosseri's advice; we read it as the test of the idea.
  4. **Likes per reach** [S1-07]: we read it as follower satisfaction.
- **Reference points (YouTube and TikTok, not Instagram benchmarks):**
  - Galloway's 2023 Shorts study: Shorts under 60% viewed-vs-swiped rarely did well, and the best sat at 70-90%. He says to watch the metric but not obsess [S4-07] (B, YouTube only).
  - Hoyos says her scroll-through rate is about 85%, against "the average" of about 70% [S4-16] (B, spoken).
  - In the 347-user TikTok study, 45% of views were watched to the end [S3-19] (A).
- **One variable per test:** same idea with two hooks as two trials, or the same reel in Slovene and English as two trials.
- **Volume:**
  - One vidIQ panelist cited an unverified study in which successful Shorts channels had published at least 200 Shorts [S4-31] (LEAD).
  - TikTok says its success cases show higher views for publishers who post several times a day [S2-36] (A, anecdote).
  - One speaker advises publishing at about 80% and iterating [S4-30] (B).

### 2.8 Local business (café, Kaval, clients)
- **What TikTok's food-business ads guide recommends** [S5-25, S5-26] (A, guidance, no data):
  - behind the scenes, process and staff, to build trust
  - recipe or process reveals "consistently perform well"
  - an 80/20 split of value and entertainment versus promotion
  - a hook in the first 3 s
  - local creator partnerships for foot traffic
- A small Slovenian survey (70 completed responses, convenience sample, self-reported) found entertaining, creative and educational TikTok content engaged most, and product-only promotion engaged less [S5-38] (A, very weak).
- **Do reels bring visits?**
  - The two TikTok-published visit-lift cases we found are paid campaigns by chains: McDonald's Netherlands +6.6% net store visits against a synthetic-control baseline [S5-22] (A), and Outback +12% visits with out-of-home coupons, method not given [S5-23] (A). Neither is Instagram Reels or organic.
  - A restaurant-consulting vendor's page cites one operator with 310,000 plays and 61 attributable orders in 90 days [S5-42] (LEAD).
  - So the café measures its own result: a promo code or a "say you saw the reel" offer, counted at the till (our method).
- **Location:**
  - Instagram Map launched in the US in Aug 2025 with "more global availability soon". Slovenia's status is unknown, and we found no statement that location tags raise reach [S1-52, S5-32] (A).
  - TikTok Nearby launched in France, Germany, Italy and the UK in Dec 2025. Slovenia wasn't named [S5-27] (A).
  - Tag the location anyway, since it costs nothing.
- **Reposts:** native Instagram reposts are credited to the original poster and recommended to friends' and followers' feeds [S1-71, S5-31] (A). Using them for guests' café reels is our inference. Re-uploading a downloaded copy risks the repost penalty [S1-21].

## 3. Popular claims that did not survive
| Claim | What we found |
|---|---|
| "85% of videos are watched without sound" | The 2016 Digiday article cites publishers' own data (LittleThings, Mic, PopSugar at 50-80%) and an agency, with no independent measurement [S3-25]. Meta's Reels-ads page says over 75% of Reels views are sound on [S3-08] |
| "Hashtags boost reach" | Mosseri says they don't significantly increase reach [S1-38, S1-39]. Instagram is limiting posts to five [S1-40]. Its undated FAQ still says relevant trending ones can help [S1-41] |
| "Hidden keywords help TikTok SEO" | TikTok calls it a myth that can get a video flagged as spam [S2-19] |
| "Post at 6 pm" | None of the Instagram or YouTube pages we opened gives a clock time; both point to your own audience data [S1-49, S2-61]. TikTok: none found |
| "The ideal reel is N seconds" | No platform page we opened names one [S1-11]. Galloway's YouTube data point to hold, not length alone [S4-09] |
| "Faster cuts raise retention" | We found no study that measures cut frequency against retention |
| "Proven hook formulas" | No A/B data on hook types found. All formula lists are LEAD [S4-36..S4-47] |
| "Attention span is 1.7 s" | That's Facebook IQ's average time with a piece of feed content (published Apr 2016, data from 2014-2015), not video retention [S3-01] |

## 4. Still unknown (how we'll find out)
- **Slovene vs English reach:** there's no Slovene-specific data. The only quantified local-vs-English comparison is a vendor's LinkedIn corpus for eight other countries [S5-21] (B, vendor). Test: paired trial reels, SL and EN versions of the same video, compared on views and shares.
- **Which hook type works for Stepan's audience:** two hooks, one video, two trials. Compare skip rate if trials show it; otherwise views, shares and watch time.
- **Slovene keyword search on Instagram:** it was English-focused in 2021 [S1-44]. Test: search our own keywords a week after posting.
- **Signal weights:** no platform page we opened publishes them (see the UNKNOWN notes in `raw/S2.md`). Don't chase them.
- **Café visits from reels:** a promo code at the till.
