# Pitch, one-pager and Q&A

## Story (one slide per beat; cut beats before cutting evidence)
1. **Their problem in their words**: quote the brief.
2. **The insight**: one sentence they had not framed this way, with one sourced number.
3. **One concrete case**: the beachhead customer or project with names, dates, who decides.
4. **The offer / mechanism**: the spine idea and how it works for that case.
5. **Why them, why now**: what only the asker can do, and the window.
6. **First 3 steps, starting Monday**: each with owner role, cost and a leading-indicator KPI.
7. **Numbers that hold**: 3-5 numbers, each from a USE row, source on the slide.
8. **Assumptions to confirm with you**: the 2-3 things only the company can confirm. This shows honesty and wins Q&A.
9. **The ask / close.**

At most one market-size slide, and only if it justifies the target. Script it at about 120-130 words per minute and plan for 90% of the time limit. Agree a hand signal for "one minute left".

## Numbers
- Only from USE or USE CORRECTED rows of the claim sheet, copied by claim ID. ASSUMPTION rows say "we estimate".
- Verbal company statements: "as you told us on <date>", never as our fact.
- Run `ledger.py numcheck` on the final script, slides text and one-pager.

## One-pager
Same order as the pitch. Team name, company, members and the file-naming rule exactly as the organiser asked. Export to PDF and check it opens. Submit at the internal deadline and save the confirmation.

## Q&A drill
- Generate 25 hard questions: 5 per evaluator persona (engineer, sales, finance/management, organiser/professor) plus 5 "gotcha" questions on our weakest claims (from CHECK FIRST and ASSUMPTION rows).
- A 20-second answer to each: answer, number with source, or "we don't know; here is how we'd find out in a week".
- Assign who answers which topic. Never let two people answer at once.
- Keep a sources dossier (one page or a link) ready to show if they ask "where is that from?".

## Rehearsal
Two timed full runs with the team acting as the jury. After each: cut whatever ran over, fix any number someone stumbled on, re-time.
