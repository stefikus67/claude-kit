# Shot structures per video type

**Render status:** the pipeline does cuts, captions, punch-ins (115% on every second sentence), B-roll overlays (`overlays[]` in edl.json, placed by hand from the transcript times) and the English version. Not yet: music and beat sync, or automatic B-roll choice. Never use CapCut (its watermark is penalised); Instagram's Edits app has no watermark (A: S1-55).

The timings are starting points (our design, built on the rules in `rules.md`). Change them to fit the idea. Every type ends right after its payoff.

## 1. Talking head (opinion, explainer, student / business life)
| Beat | s | Shot | Audio | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | Close-up, face centred, eyes to lens, movement already on (walking, gesture, an object raised) | Hook line, no "hey guys" | Hook text, 3-6 words, upper third |
| Promise / setup | 3-8 | Same, or a punch-in cut | Why it matters to the viewer ("you") | none, or one keyword |
| Beats 1-3 (list or but/therefore chain) | 8-40 | Alternate the A-roll with a punch-in (110-120% crop) every new sentence; B-roll of the thing named | One idea per beat; each beat opens the next question | Number or keyword per beat |
| Payoff | 40-50 | Back to the face, or the reveal shot | The answer, the result, the twist | Payoff in 3-5 words |
| End / loop | 50-55 | Cut on the last word | Last line that runs into the hook (loop) or a specific CTA (comment a keyword) | none |

- Avoid a **static** talking head (TikTok ads guidance, A). Move, punch in, cut to B-roll.
- Film it as one take with "še enkrat" restarts. The pipeline removes the retakes.

## 2. Vlog with B-roll (a day, an event, a place)
| Beat | s | Shot | Audio | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | The most striking moment of the day **first** (in medias res) | Voiceover line or live sound | 3-6 words |
| Context | 3-6 | Wide establishing shot | "Today I ..." in one sentence | Place or time |
| Sequence | 6-40 | B-roll clips of 1-2.5 s, each one new information; on the beat if there's music (librosa beat grid, planned) | Voiceover tells one story while the images add a second layer (B, dual narrative S4-21) | Sparse |
| (after filming) | | **Pick the hook and the peak from the footage:** the director watches the contact sheet and transcript and names the strongest 3 s for the opening | | |
| Peak | 40-50 | The best moment, slightly longer | Payoff line | Payoff |
| End | 50-55 | Close-up or a return to the first shot (loop) | | |

- Build a B-roll library: five 5-7 s clips per routine, filed by activity (LEAD, S4-53). Keep it in `intake/broll/<topic>/`.

## 3. Business promo (café, Kaval, client)
| Beat | s | Shot | Audio | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | The process at its most visual: pouring, steam, cake cut, a hand putting down the cup, close and in motion | Natural sound plus one spoken line, or text only | Benefit or curiosity, 3-6 words |
| Process / behind the scenes | 3-20 | 3-6 quick process shots; staff faces (with their OK) | Short voiceover: what, who, why | Item and price only if Stepan approves |
| Proof / place | 20-35 | The room, the light, a guest's reaction (with consent), the view of Tivoli | | Place |
| Offer / CTA | 35-45 | Product hero shot | One specific CTA: "Pokaži ta reel na blagajni" (code), or the opening hours | CTA |
| End | 45-50 | Logo (own logo is fine) or a loop | | |

- 80% value or entertainment, 20% promotion (TikTok food guide, A).
- Post as a **Collab** with the partner account (supplier, artist, event) when it makes sense (A/B).
- Every offer, price or promise needs Stepan's OK before posting.
- Measure visits with a code or a till question; reach alone doesn't prove the café gained anything.
- People on camera: staff have given their OK; Stepan asks each guest after filming. A guest without a yes is cut before posting. No minors without a parent's consent.
