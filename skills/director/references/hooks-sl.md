# Hook patterns (all LEAD: no A/B data on hook types exists in what we opened)

**Source of the pattern list:**
- the `rediumvex/viral-hooks-skill` catalogue (MIT, provenance unverifiable) [S4-36..S4-47]
- practitioner guidance [S4-08, S4-11, S4-12]
- one headline study: reveal a **middling** amount of information [S3-44]

**How to use:**
- Write 3 variants per script, mixing one stop-scroll hook (negation, specificity, question) with one retention hook (story, confession, "you").
- Each one has a **visual**, a **spoken line** and **on-screen text** of 3-6 words.
- Keep the spoken hook under about 12 words.
- Rotate patterns: recognisable formulas are claimed to wear out [S4-34, S4-35] (LEAD).
- The Slovene examples below are templates. Proofread the final text (prompt-forge section 5), and check every fact in a hook before posting.

| Pattern | Template | Slovene example (tikanje, young audience) |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity gap | "Nobody tells you X about Y" | "Tega ti o študiju na EF nihče ne pove." |
| Contrarian | "Stop doing X" | "Nehaj se učiti dan pred izpitom. Takole raje naredi." |
| Specificity (number) | "N: that's how many..." | "4,50 € in 12 minut: toliko stane moje kosilo med predavanji." |
| Question | "Why does X keep happening?" | "Zakaj je kava v kavarni dražja kot v trgovini? Razčlenimo." |
| Story, in medias res | "Last week X happened" | "Prejšnji teden se je v kavarni zgodilo nekaj, česar nisem pričakoval." |
| Myth-buster | "Myth: X. Reality: Y" | "Mit: v Ljubljani ni dobre kave. Resnica: …" |
| Confession | "I'll admit it: I…" | "Priznam: prvi mesec sem vse delal narobe." |
| "You" hook | "If you X, watch this" | "Če študiraš in delaš hkrati, poglej tole." |
| List | "N things I wish I knew at X" | "3 stvari, ki bi jih rad vedel pred prvim letnikom." |
| Visual first | No line; the image is the hook (pour, reveal, before/after) | Text only: "Kako nastane naša torta" |

## Checks for every hook
1. Does the first frame alone tell what this is about? (thumbnail test, B)
2. Does it work muted? (B)
3. Is the payoff named but not given away? (middling information, A for headlines)
4. Is it true? A hook that the video doesn't pay off costs trust (the title/thumbnail promise must match, B, S4-05).
